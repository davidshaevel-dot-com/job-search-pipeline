The Opportunity

As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you will be part of an Agile development team where you will support the design, development, modification, and evaluation of signals intelligence, radar, and communications prototype development systems, related software systems, and development environments for our Navy customer. You will be instrumental in the engineering and advanced research and development associated with designing of products during design, development, integration, and acceptance. Your area of work will include serving as a technical expert for efforts related to both DevOps configuration, automation, and deployment as well as acting as a Site Reliability Engineer.

Responsibilities
• Planning and executing projects or phases of projects to include customer interface, requirements development, design reviews, and technical interchanges, and all aspects of design, development, demonstration, and evaluation.
• Lead CI/CD pipeline setup and maintenance
• Implement virtual cloud deployment/test environments
• Provide continuous deployment
• Utilize some or all the following Ansible, Jenkins, Artifactory, Python, Kubernetes, Helm, Terraform, Packer, Vagrant to build, package, deploy, and analyze artifacts, applications, containers, and virtual machines to various environments.
• Automate dependency management
• Implement CM strategies
• Implement a promotional release strategy
• Provide multiple enclave support
• Support cyber security scans, lockdowns, and vulnerability analysis
• Provide and maintain configuration-controlled build containers
• Implement dynamic creation of builder nodes for Jenkins
• Facilitate OTA updates of fielded systems
• Administer Atlassian Jira, Confluence, and Bitbucket or portions thereof
• Provide associated design documentation
• Mentor teammates as needed
